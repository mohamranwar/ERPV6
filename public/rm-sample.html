The ±15% — what it should mean
This is the question I could not explain well. Here it is as a picture instead.

Option A — uncertainty band recommended

—
The projection is the 4-month average. The ±15% is shown as a range around it, not baked into the number.

Same every time you open the screen. Press "Regenerate scenario" above — this number will not move. You can export it Tuesday and Thursday and reconcile them.

Option B — per-month varied what you asked for

—
Each month gets its own random figure inside ±15%, so the line looks realistic rather than flat.

Press "Regenerate scenario" and watch this number change. That is the whole problem: October is 1,240 now and 1,080 after a refresh. Nobody changed anything.
⚠
Why I keep pushing on this If a projected number can change on its own, it cannot be compared, reconciled, or sent to a supplier. This project already spent a session removing four separate places where the dashboard invented figures — a random multiplier is the same problem wearing a different hat.
✓
The middle option, if you want the varied shape Keep per-month variation, but derive it from the material code + month instead of a random number. October for fluff pulp then always lands on the same figure — varied across months, stable across refreshes. That is the "Per-month varied" mode above: it stays put when you press Regenerate, because it is computed, not rolled. Only "Regenerate scenario" changes it — and that is a deliberate what-if, not a page load.
